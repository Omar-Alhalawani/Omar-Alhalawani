## About Me

**Signals.** I'm a third-year **B.Eng. Computer Systems Engineering (Honours Co-op)** student at **Carleton University**, interested in the layers of computing closest to the hardware: **ASIC design verification, RTL, digital systems, embedded computing and engineering automation**.

I completed a **Summer 2026 ASIC Engineer I co-op at Ciena** on the Digital Verification Methodology / Methodology & Automation team, and **will return to Ciena for a Winter 2027 co-op**. My experience includes evaluating **pyuvm and cocotb** for verification workflows, developing and validating self-checking testbenches, investigating fault-injection and regression behavior, and using Python to analyze Makefile-based compile flows.

Beyond industry, I've worked as a **Teaching Assistant** for Digital Systems and Programming, supported **embedded hardware and research instrumentation** at Carleton, and founded **Alhalawani Tech Solutions**, an electronics repair business. I'm an **Ontario Professional Engineers Scholarship** recipient and currently serve as **Vice President Internal** of the Carleton Muslim Students' Association.
