## About Me

I am a third-year **Honours Computer Systems Engineering Co-op student at Carleton University** and an **ASIC Engineering Co-op at Ciena**.

I enjoy building systems at the boundary between hardware and software, including real-time computer vision, embedded devices, RTL design and verification, robotics, automation tooling, and Linux-based systems.

My favourite projects are the ones where timing, hardware limitations, system integration, and debugging all matter.

- Working with **Python, SystemVerilog, UVM, pyuvm, Linux, Git, and verification workflows**
- Building embedded systems using **C, C++, Raspberry Pi, ESP32, Arduino, sensors, and motor control**
- Exploring **ROS 2 robotics, real-time systems, FPGA design, and autonomous systems**
- Turning discontinued or proprietary hardware into open and maintainable systems
